Title: Simple House Vinaigrette

Ingredients:
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp honey
- 1 pinch garlic powder
- salt to taste
- black pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Add all ingredients into a small jar.
2. Seal the lid tightly and shake vigorously for 15–20 seconds until emulsified.
