Title: Classic Garlic Aioli

Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. In a small bowl, combine the mayonnaise and minced garlic.
2. Stir in the fresh lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
3. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving to let the flavors meld.
