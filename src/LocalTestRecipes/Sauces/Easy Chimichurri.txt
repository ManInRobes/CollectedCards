Title: Easy Chimichurri

Ingredients:
- 1 cup fresh parsley (finely chopped)
- 3 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- salt to taste

Instructions:
1. Combine chopped parsley, garlic, oregano, red pepper flakes, and salt in a bowl.
2. Stir in red wine vinegar and olive oil until combined.
3. Rest at room temperature for 15 minutes before using.
