Title: Red Wine Braised Short Ribs

Ingredients:
- 4 lb bone-in beef short ribs, trimmed of excess surface fat
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 large yellow onions, diced
- 3 large carrots, peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks
- 3 stalks celery, chopped into 1-inch pieces
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed and peeled
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cups dry full-bodied red wine
- 3 cups low-sodium beef stock
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 bay leaves
- 1/2 cup fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp lemon zest

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 325 F and pat the beef short ribs completely dry with paper towels, then season generously on all sides with kosher salt and black pepper.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed Dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering, then sear the short ribs in two batches for 3 to 4 minutes per side until a deep brown crust forms.
3. Transfer the browned ribs to a sheet pan and pour off all but 2 tablespoons of rendered fat from the pot.
4. Lower the heat to medium, add the diced onions, carrots, and celery, and cook for 8 to 10 minutes until softened and lightly browned around the edges.
5. Stir in the smashed garlic and tomato paste, cooking constantly for 2 minutes until the paste darkens to a brick-red color.
6. Pour in the dry red wine to deglaze the pot, scraping up all browned bits from the bottom, and boil for 6 to 8 minutes until the liquid reduces by half.
7. Return the short ribs and any accumulated juices to the Dutch oven, nestling them bone-side up into the vegetables, then pour in the beef stock until the ribs are nearly submerged.
8. Tuck the thyme, rosemary, and bay leaves into the liquid, bring to a gentle simmer on the stovetop, cover tightly with a lid, and transfer to the oven for 2.5 to 3 hours until the meat is fork-tender.
9. Skim excess fat from the surface of the braising liquid, discard the spent herb stems and bay leaves, and finish with a gremolata tossed together from the chopped parsley and lemon zest before serving.
