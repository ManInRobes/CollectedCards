Title: Classic Caesar Salad

Ingredients:
- 1 head romaine lettuce (chopped)
- 1/2 cup caesar dressing
- 1 cup croutons
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese

Instructions:
1. Wash and chop the romaine lettuce into bite-sized pieces.
2. Toss the lettuce in a large bowl with the Caesar dressing until evenly coated.
3. Top with croutons and freshly grated parmesan cheese before serving.
