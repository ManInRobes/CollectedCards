Title: Sesame Ginger Slaw

Ingredients:
- 3 cups shredded cabbage mix
- 1/2 cup grated carrots
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp grated ginger

Instructions:
1. Whisk soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, honey, and ginger together until smooth.
2. Combine cabbage and carrots in a salad bowl.
3. Drizzle sauce over the slaw and toss to coat thoroughly.
