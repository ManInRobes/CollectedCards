Title: Mediterranean Cucumber Tomato Salad

Ingredients:
- 2 diced cucumbers
- 2 diced tomatoes
- 1/4 sliced red onion
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- oregano to taste
- salt to taste

Instructions:
1. Combine cucumbers, tomatoes, and red onion in a bowl.
2. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, dried oregano, and salt together.
3. Pour dressing over the vegetables, fold in crumbled feta, and toss gently.
