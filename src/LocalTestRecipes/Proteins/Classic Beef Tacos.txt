Title: Classic Beef Tacos

Ingredients:
1 lb ground beef
1 packet taco seasoning
8 taco shells
1 cup shredded lettuce

Instructions:
1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add taco seasoning and water according to packet instructions, simmering until thickened.
3. Assemble tacos with beef, lettuce, and your favorite toppings.
