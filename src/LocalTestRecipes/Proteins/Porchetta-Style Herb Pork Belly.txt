Title: Porchetta-Style Herb Pork Belly

Ingredients:
- 4 lb skin-on or fat-cap pork belly, scored in a diamond pattern
- 1 tbsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp fennel seeds, toasted and coarsely crushed
- 6 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 2 tbsp fresh rosemary, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh sage, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves, chopped
- 1 tbsp lemon zest
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 cup dry white wine

Instructions:
1. Place the pork belly flesh-side up on a cutting board and score the meat in a shallow crosshatch pattern, taking care not to cut through the bottom fat cap.
2. Combine the toasted crushed fennel seeds, minced garlic, rosemary, sage, thyme, lemon zest, kosher salt, black pepper, and olive oil into a thick paste.
3. Rub the herb paste thoroughly into every crevice of the scored pork belly, then roll the belly tightly lengthwise and tie every 1.5 inches with butcher's twine.
4. Set the tied roast on a wire rack inside a rimmed sheet pan and refrigerate uncovered overnight for at least 12 hours to dry out the exterior.
5. Preheat the oven to 300 F, pour the dry white wine into the bottom of the sheet pan beneath the rack, and roast the pork for 2.5 hours until tender.
6. Increase the oven temperature to 450 F and roast for an additional 20 to 25 minutes until the exterior turns golden, blistered, and crisp.
7. Rest the porchetta on a carving board for 20 minutes before snipping the twine and slicing into thick rounds.
