Title: Garlic Butter Steak Bites

Ingredients:
- 1 lb sirloin steak (cubed)
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 cloves garlic (minced)
- salt, pepper, parsley

Instructions:
1. Season beef cubes generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat a skillet over high heat and sear beef in 1 tbsp oil for 3-4 minutes until browned.
3. Lower heat, add butter and garlic, and toss for 1 minute until fragrant. Garnish with parsley and serve.
