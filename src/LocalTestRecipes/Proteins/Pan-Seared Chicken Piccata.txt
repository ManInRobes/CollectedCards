Title: Pan-Seared Chicken Piccata

Ingredients:
- 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts, halved horizontally into cutlets
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken stock
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 3 tbsp capers, drained and rinsed
- 1/4 cup fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped

Instructions:
1. Place the chicken cutlets between two sheets of plastic wrap and pound gently with a meat mallet to an even 1/4-inch thickness.
2. Season both sides of the cutlets with kosher salt and black pepper, then dredge lightly in the all-purpose flour, shaking off any excess.
3. Heat the olive oil and 1 tablespoon of unsalted butter in a large stainless steel skillet over medium-high heat until foaming subsides.
4. Sear the chicken cutlets in two batches for 2.5 to 3 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through, then transfer to a warm platter.
5. Lower the heat to medium, add the minced garlic, and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant without browning.
6. Deglaze the skillet with the dry white wine, scraping up all browned bits, and simmer for 2 minutes until nearly evaporated.
7. Pour in the chicken stock, fresh lemon juice, and drained capers, bringing the sauce to a brisk simmer for 4 to 5 minutes until reduced by one-third.
8. Turn off the heat, swirl in the remaining 3 tablespoons of cold unsalted butter and the chopped parsley until emulsified, and spoon the sauce over the chicken.
