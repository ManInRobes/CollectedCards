Title: Simple Beef Stir-Fry

Ingredients:
- 1 lb flank steak (sliced thin)
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder

Instructions:
1. Whisk soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, and garlic powder in a small bowl.
2. Stir-fry beef in a hot pan for 3-4 minutes until browned, then remove.
3. Cook broccoli in the same pan with 2 tbsp water until tender-crisp. Return beef to pan, pour in sauce, and toss for 1 minute to coat.
