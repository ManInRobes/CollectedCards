Title: Chicken Chile Verde

Ingredients:
- 3 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, trimmed
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil
- 1 1/2 lbs fresh tomatillos, husked and rinsed
- 2 poblano peppers, halved and seeded
- 2 jalapeno peppers, stemmed
- 1 large white onion, quartered
- 5 cloves garlic, peeled
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 cup fresh cilantro leaves and tender stems
- 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven broiler to high with a rack positioned 6 inches below the heating element, and line a rimmed baking sheet with foil.
2. Arrange the husked tomatillos, halved poblanos, jalapenos, quartered white onion, and garlic cloves on the baking sheet and broil for 8 to 10 minutes, turning once, until charred and blistered in spots.
3. Transfer the charred vegetables and all pan juices into a blender along with the cilantro, dried oregano, and 1 cup of the chicken broth, then blend until smooth.
4. Season the chicken thighs evenly with kosher salt, ground cumin, and black pepper.
5. Heat the cooking oil in a wide Dutch oven over medium-high heat and sear the chicken thighs in batches for 3 minutes per side until golden brown.
6. Pour the blended green tomatillo salsa and remaining 1 cup of chicken broth into the pot, scraping the bottom to release any browned bits.
7. Bring the mixture to a boil, reduce the heat to low, cover partially, and simmer gently for 35 to 40 minutes until the chicken shreds easily with two forks.
8. Shred the chicken directly in the sauce, stir in the fresh lime juice, and simmer uncovered for 5 more minutes to thicken slightly before serving.
