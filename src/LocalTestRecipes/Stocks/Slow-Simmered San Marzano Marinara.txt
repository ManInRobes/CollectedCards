Title: Slow-Simmered San Marzano Marinara

Ingredients:
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
- 2 tbsp double-concentrated tomato paste
- 56 oz canned whole peeled San Marzano tomatoes, crushed by hand
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 3 sprigs fresh basil
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter

Instructions:
1. Warm the extra-virgin olive oil in a heavy-bottomed saucepan or Dutch oven over medium heat until fluid and fragrant.
2. Add the finely diced yellow onion and cook slowly for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until translucent and soft without browning.
3. Stir in the thinly sliced garlic and crushed red pepper flakes, cooking for 90 seconds until aromatic.
4. Add the tomato paste and stir continuously for 2 minutes to caramelize the sugars and deepen the flavor.
5. Pour in the hand-crushed San Marzano tomatoes along with their juices, rinsing each can with 1/4 cup of water and adding it to the pot.
6. Season with kosher salt, black pepper, and dried oregano, then submerge the whole fresh basil sprigs into the sauce.
7. Bring to a gentle bubble, lower the heat to the lowest setting, and simmer partially covered for 45 to 60 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes to prevent scorching.
8. Remove the spent basil stems, swirl in the unsalted butter until glossy, and adjust salt to taste before cooling or portioning.
