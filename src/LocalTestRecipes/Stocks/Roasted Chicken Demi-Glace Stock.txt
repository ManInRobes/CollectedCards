Title: Roasted Chicken Demi-Glace Stock

Ingredients:
- 4 lbs chicken backs, wings, and necks
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 large yellow onions, roughly chopped
- 3 large carrots, scrubbed and chopped
- 3 stalks celery, chopped
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 head garlic, halved crosswise
- 12 cups cold water
- 6 sprigs fresh parsley
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp whole black peppercorns

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 425 F and toss the chicken bones with the neutral oil on a large heavy roasting pan.
2. Roast the bones for 35 minutes, then scatter the chopped onions, carrots, and celery around the pan and roast for another 20 minutes until deeply golden brown.
3. Brush the tomato paste over the roasted bones and vegetables and return to the oven for 5 minutes to caramelize.
4. Transfer all roasted bones and vegetables into a large stockpot, pour 1 cup of water into the hot roasting pan to scrape up the fond, and pour that liquid into the pot.
5. Add the halved head of garlic and the remaining 11 cups of cold water so the solids are covered by 2 inches.
6. Set over medium-high heat and bring just to a bare simmer, skimming off any gray foam that rises to the surface during the first 15 minutes.
7. Add the parsley, thyme, bay leaves, and black peppercorns, then maintain a gentle, barely bubbling simmer uncovered for 4 hours.
8. Strain the stock through a fine-mesh sieve lined with cheesecloth, discard the solids, and chill rapidly in shallow containers for line storage.
